DocuSnap ("we", "our", or "the app") is a document and image utility application developed by Debashis Kar Suvra. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. By using DocuSnap, you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to provide and improve the app:

  • Crash & diagnostic data — collected automatically via Firebase Crashlytics (stack traces, device model, OS version, app version). This helps us fix bugs.
  • Advertising data — Google AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and interaction data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • Usage analytics — Firebase may collect anonymized session and event data (screen views, feature usage) to help us understand how the app is used.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information directly.

3. File & Document Handling

All files you process in DocuSnap (PDFs, images, scanned documents) are handled entirely on your device. We do not upload your files to any server. Files are read from and saved to your device's local storage only.

Always keep backups of your important files. DocuSnap is not responsible for data loss resulting from processing operations.

4. OCR & ML Kit

The OCR (text extraction) feature uses Google ML Kit, which runs entirely on-device. Your images are not sent to Google servers for OCR processing. ML Kit uses the on-device model only.

OCR accuracy is not guaranteed. Always verify extracted text before relying on it for important documents.

5. Third-Party Services

DocuSnap uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Crashlytics, Analytics) – crash reporting & analytics
  • Google AdMob – advertising
  • Google ML Kit – on-device OCR & document scanning
  • Google Play Billing – in-app purchases

We recommend reviewing their privacy policies for details on data collected by these services.

6. Advertising

DocuSnap displays banner and interstitial ads via Google AdMob. Ads may be personalized based on your interests. You can opt out of personalized ads via Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

Upgrading to DocuSnap Pro removes all ads and stops AdMob data collection entirely.

7. Data Retention

Crash reports and analytics are retained by Firebase according to Google's retention policies (typically 90 days for Crashlytics, 14 months for Analytics). We do not retain any other user data on our servers.

8. Children's Privacy

DocuSnap is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

PDF password protection in DocuSnap uses RC4-128 encryption, which is not considered cryptographically strong by modern standards. For documents requiring high security, use a dedicated encryption tool.

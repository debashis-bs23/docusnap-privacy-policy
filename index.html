Privacy Policy

App: DocuSnap – Document & Image Utility
Developer: Debashis
Effective date: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DocuSnap ("the App") handles information when you use it on your Android device. We take your privacy seriously and collect as little data as possible.

1. Information We Collect

DocuSnap processes files, images, and documents that you explicitly select. This processing happens entirely on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or store your files on any server.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of DocuSnap displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the following to serve relevant ads:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising Identifier)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Approximate location (country/region, derived from IP address)
  • App interaction data (ad impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time via Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation.

Upgrading to DocuSnap Pro removes all ads and stops AdMob data collection.

3. In-App Purchases

DocuSnap offers a one-time in-app purchase ("Remove Ads – Pro") processed through the Google Play Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Purchase records are managed by Google Play in accordance with Google Play's policies.

We store a local flag on your device to remember your Pro status. This flag is not transmitted to any server.

4. Camera & Storage Permissions

Camera: Used only when you activate the document scanner feature. Camera access is not used for any other purpose and no images are captured without your explicit action.

Storage: Used to read files you select for processing (PDF, images) and to save output files to your device. We do not access files you have not explicitly chosen.

5. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not create user accounts or collect personal information such as name, email, or phone number.
  • We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • We do not share your files or documents with any third party.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs beyond what AdMob includes.

6. Data Retention

All file processing is on-device and temporary. Output files are saved to the location you choose on your device. We do not retain copies of your files.

7. Children's Privacy

DocuSnap is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party SDKs, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob – advertising
  • Google Play Billing – in-app purchases
  • Google ML Kit – on-device document scanning (no data leaves your device)

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
